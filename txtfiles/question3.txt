What goes in the <head> of a page versus the <body>, and who sees each?
The <head> only holds stuff your browser needs to run the program while <body> holds the things your vistors can 
see.

What is the difference between running your page at localhost:3000 and at <your-IP>:8080?
The difference is that running it on localhost is running the webpage on only your computer while running your
webpage at your IP:8080 can be shown to anyone.

In your own words, what does push do and what does pull do in today's loop?
Push puts our code on github saving our changes while pull takes what we pushed on git to pull it to the webpage.

Why do we test on localhost:3000 before pushing to production?
We test on localhost, so that we make sure everything looks good to us before making a permanent change to our client-side webpage.
